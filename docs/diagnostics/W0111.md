# boyko-W0111 - a target is armed but nothing accepts it

## What happened

A target is enabled, it produced no records, and **no active sink would have accepted any**:

```text
boyko-W0111: target render is armed at debug but no active sink accepts it: its silence is not evidence
```

Its census row reads `status=UNPROVEN(unsunk)` rather than `UNPROVEN`.

## Why

The two silences are identical in the file and mean opposite things. One says the subsystem had
nothing to report. The other says nobody was listening. A game enables a category, sees an empty
log, and concludes "clean" - the vacuous gate this whole diagnostics corpus exists to remove,
wearing a new costume.

The census can tell them apart because the sink policy table is still there to ask: state, floor
and target filter, per sink. It asks at the target's **own ceiling**, not at a fixed level - a
target armed at `Warn` whose sink floor is `Warn` is perfectly sunk, and testing at `Trace` would
report every correctly-configured pair as broken.

`Once`, because the condition is a **configuration**, not an event. Every later unsunk row has the
same cause, and one misconfiguration must not become a storm of reports about it - the argument
`W0501` records for the query-type table and `W0114` for the index budget. It names the target,
because "something was unsunk" leaves the reader diffing a census against a sink policy by hand.

## How to fix

- **Check the sink's floor**: `slot::floor(slot)` below the target's level silently discards
  everything that target emits. A file at `Warn` and a target at `Debug` produce exactly this.
- **Check the sink's filter**: `slot::filter_admits(slot, target)`. `set_only_target` narrows to
  one target and excludes the other 255 - including, easily, the one being chased.
- **Check the sink's state**: `Paused` and `Off` both accept nothing. `Paused` keeps the file.
- **Or accept the answer**: if you did not intend to capture this target, the row is telling you
  the truth and the fix is to stop arming it.
