# boyko-W9218 — a telemetry quantile subscription was refused past the cap

## What happened

More zones asked for windowed quantiles than the per-session cap allows:

```text
boyko-W9218: a telemetry quantile subscription was refused past the per-session cap; the zone
streams without a median or a p95
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_app/src/profiling/stream.rs` through
`boyko_diag::loss::DiagFlag::TelemetryZonesRefused`, emitted by
`crates/boyko_ecs/src/ecs/core/profiling/diag.rs` (`report_telemetry_zones_refused`).

**The refused zone still streams.** Its `count`, `total`, `min` and `max` are in every window
record, because those are O(1) folds. What it does not get is `median` and `p95`, and its record
says so with a flag rather than with a zero — a reader calling `WindowRec::quantiles()` gets `None`,
never `(0, 0)`.

## Why there is a cap at all

`count` / `total` / `min` / `max` are folds: one pass, no memory, no ordering. `median` and `p95`
are not. Each one needs a **strided gather of the whole retained window** — 121 values out of
frame-major columns — **plus a sort**, per zone. At a few hundred subscribed zones that is hundreds
of gathers across a multi-megabyte working set and hundreds of sorts, synchronously, inside a frame.

That term is the telemetry window's dominant cost, and the cap is what bounds it:

| Term | Budget, p95 |
|---|---|
| `__telemetry_reduce` at the cap | ≤ 150 µs |
| `__telemetry_write` | ≤ 200 µs |
| **their sum** | **≤ 350 µs** |

350 µs once per window is about 2.1 % of one frame in 121. It is a *spike*, stated as one rather
than amortised — and it is below this machine's own measured decidability floor, which is to say
below what this project's instruments can resolve.

## Why it is a refusal and not a clamp

A silently dropped subscription would give a zone a `median` field filled with something, and
whatever that something was would be read as a measurement. A refusal plus a flag on the record says
exactly what is known: this zone's distribution was not computed.

## Why it is a warning and not an error

Nothing failed. A configuration asked for more than the budget allows and got as much as the budget
allows. It is a warning rather than silence because a reader looking for a p95 that is not there
needs to know it was **refused** rather than **absent because the zone never ran**.

## Why it fires once

`RatePolicy::Once`. Subscriptions are made at setup, so a configuration that overshoots by one and a
configuration that overshoots by three hundred produce the same single line. The count of refusals
is a counter, and the counter is what says how far over the configuration is.

## How to fix

- Subscribe fewer zones to quantiles. The natural set is the frame's few dominant zones, not every
  zone the build declares.
- Everything else still streams; a zone's shape over a whole session is retention tier C's job
  (`Profiler::subscribe_histogram`), which is a fixed-cost histogram rather than a per-window sort.
