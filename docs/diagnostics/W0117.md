# boyko-W0117 - the in-frame view is short, and the file is not

## What happened

A drain pass refused frames to the ECS handoff ring:

```text
boyko-W0117: the ECS handoff refused 812 frames (41724 bytes) this pass; the in-frame view is short and the byte sinks are not
```

## Why it is a warning and not an error

**The byte sinks already have every one of those records.** Only the in-frame view - the console
widget, the overlay, anything reading `LogRing` - is short. Nothing is lost from the log file; what
is lost is the ability of an on-screen reader to see it *this frame*.

That distinction is the entire reason the code exists. A reader who notices a gap in a console
widget needs to be able to tell "the engine stopped reporting" from "the widget could not keep up",
and those two look identical on screen.

## Why the count is per PASS, not per refusal

A formatting storm refuses thousands. A report per refusal would be the storm again in a second
channel, which is the failure mode `W0501` and `W0114` are both shaped to avoid. So the drain
reports **once per pass, carrying that pass's delta** - consecutive reports add up to the total
rather than each restating it, so a reader can see a storm stop.

`LogCensus.lossy` is set, and the refused frames count as `LossClass::Sink`.

## How to fix

- **Reduce what reaches the ring**: raise a target's ceiling, or exclude it from the ECS sink with
  `slot::set_target(SLOT_ECS, target, false)`. An in-frame view is for what a human watches, and a
  human cannot watch a `Trace` firehose anyway.
- **Sample it**: a `sample_shift` on the noisy target thins delivery without touching the others.
- **Drain more often**: under `SinkMode::Scheduled` the handoff is emptied once per frame, so a
  pass that produces more than the ring holds will refuse regardless of ring size.
- **Do not branch gameplay on log counters.** They are lower bounds under refusal,
  schedule-dependent, and they break replay. Display and telemetry only.
