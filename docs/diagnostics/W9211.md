# boyko-W9211 — the fold's working set does not fit L1d

## What happened

The profiler armed with a `zone_stride` whose per-frame column row is larger than the fold's L1d
budget:

```text
boyko-W9211: the profiling fold's column row is 86016 B at a zone stride of 4096, over the L1d budget
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_ecs/src/ecs/core/profiling/diag.rs` (`report_working_set`), once, at
`Profiler::arm`.

## The arithmetic, because the threshold is derived and not chosen

The store is **frame-major**: one fold writes into `[frame * zone_stride + zone]` across five
columns, so the bytes it touches per frame are

```text
21 B/zone  =  total 8 + count 4 + min 4 + max 4 + label 1
```

At `zone_stride = 1024` that is **21 504 B**, and with the fold's ~9.6 KiB of sequential lane reads
(400 samples × 24 B) the working set is **30.6 KiB against a 32 KiB L1d**. It fits, and it is
*tight* — which is why 1024 is the threshold rather than a round number, and why the figure the
warning prints is the measured working set rather than the limit it crossed.

## Why it reports instead of refusing

A game may legitimately want two thousand zones and pay for them. Refusing the arm would make the
profiler decide a trade-off that belongs to the title. What the profiler owes is the number, before
the session's timings are read as if the instrument were free.

## What it costs when you exceed it

The fold's column writes start missing L1d and hitting L2. The cost is real and is the reason the
layout fork was decided the way it was, but it is a *slowdown of the instrument*, not an error in
its output — every figure stays exact. It lands in `instrument_measured`, which is reported beside
the frame time rather than subtracted from it.

## Why it fires once

`RatePolicy::Once`. The geometry is fixed at arm and const for the session, so the condition either
holds for the whole session or never.

## How to fix

- **Lower `ProfilerConfig::user_zone_budget`.** `zone_stride = ENGINE_ZONE_SLOTS + user_zone_budget`,
  and the user half is the one a title controls.
- **Accept it.** If the zones are worth their cost, the warning has done its job by making the cost
  visible; nothing else is required.
- `ENGINE_ZONE_SLOTS` is a per-profile constant that arrives with the single build axis at the joint
  rung J1. Until then it is one value in every profile, and on a `dev` build the stride is over the
  limit by construction — which is the true statement about that geometry, and is exactly what this
  code exists to say.
