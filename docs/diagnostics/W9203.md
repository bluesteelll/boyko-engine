# boyko-W9203 — profiling samples were discarded

## What happened

One of two things, and the code covers both because a reader's first question is the same either
way — *"how much of this profile is missing?"*:

- **A lane region filled.** A producer wrote faster than the fold drained, the region's
  `REGION_CAPACITY` slots were all live, and further samples were refused at the producer:

  ```text
  boyko-W9203: a profiling lane region overflowed: 412 engine and 0 user samples discarded so far
  ```

- **A producer held no lane.** A thread the lane topology could not place emitted a sample, and
  there was no row to charge it to:

  ```text
  boyko-W9203: a profiling producer holds no lane; its samples are refused and counted on the un-laned row
  ```

Raised from `crates/boyko_ecs/src/ecs/core/profiling/diag.rs` (`report_overflow`,
`report_lane_exhausted`). The second form is `boyko_diag::loss::DiagFlag::LaneExhausted` reaching
its only mouth: the substrate is diagnostically mute and raises a sticky bit, and the profiling
fold is what turns it into a code.

## Why it is a warning and not an error

Nothing is broken and no number in the artifact is *wrong*. What is true is that the window is a
**lower bound**: the figures describe the samples that survived, not the samples that happened. The
drop counters (`engine_overflow`, `user_overflow`, `unclaimed`) carry the magnitude, and a contrast
over a window with any drop refuses to resolve rather than comparing a truncated leg.

## Why the engine and the user halves are counted separately

A lane has two regions, and that is an isolation fix before it is a false-sharing one. A runaway
game scope — a zone opened per entity, per draw, per particle — fills the **user** region and drops
its own samples; `engine_overflow` stays zero and the engine's frame timings stay whole. One region
would have made a game's mistake indistinguishable from an engine regression, in the artifact whose
whole job is to tell them apart.

## Why it fires once

`RatePolicy::Once`, with a site-local latch. The condition recurs every frame under load, and a
code that repeated would drown the very records a reader needs. The *magnitude* is in the counters,
which keep climbing after the code has gone quiet — the code says **that** it happened, the counters
say **how much**.

## How to fix

- **Drain more often.** The fold runs once per frame at the top of `App::update_with_delta`. A host
  that folds less often than it produces will overflow at any capacity.
- **Emit less.** A zone at `Deep` tier that fires per entity is the usual cause. Raise its tier, or
  move it behind a scope the session does not arm.
- **Raise `REGION_CAPACITY`** — the last resort, because the sample slab is `LANE_COUNT × 2 ×
  REGION_CAPACITY × 24 B` and it is committed at arm, so it is the term that grows fastest.
- **For the un-laned form:** the thread needs `boyko_diag::lane::claim_lane()`. Pool workers and
  the host get one automatically; a thread a mod or a tool spawned does not.
