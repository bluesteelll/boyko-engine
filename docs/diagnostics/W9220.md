# boyko-W9220 - a GPU zone's declared record order was violated

## What happened

On at least one frame of a measured window, a bracketed zone did not sit where its family's
declared chain says it sits:

```text
boyko-W9220: GPU zone 11 left its declared position inside the run bracket 12 -- that frame's
brackets do not partition the interval they divide
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_app/src/profiling/reduce.rs` (`WindowReducer::observe_frame`), on the
first violating frame. The count for the whole window is in the artifact's `[order]` block.

## Why

A family declares a **chain**: the containing run bracket first, then the members in the order
their BEGIN stamps must land. Every frame is checked against it, on that frame's own raw ticks:

- each present member's BEGIN is at or after the previous present member's BEGIN (ties are legal —
  equal-tick `BOTTOM_OF_PIPE` stamps give equal offsets), and
- each present member's whole interval nests inside the run's.

When that holds, consecutive `BOTTOM_OF_PIPE` begins are prefix-completion times and the intervals
between them **partition** the run — which is the property that makes a difference of two of them a
measurement of the work between them. When it does not hold, the rows overlap and any sum or
difference over them counts some work twice and some not at all.

**The usual cause is a stage, not a site.** A `TOP_OF_PIPE` begin retires when the command is
*fetched*, so a bracket that opens downstream of unbracketed work latches early and its interval
overlaps its predecessor's. Measured on this box: three back-to-back compute dispatches summed to
1.083 / 1.025 / 1.077 of the span they were supposed to divide under `TOP`, and to
1.000 / 1.002 / 1.000 under `BOTTOM`.

**This is a COUNT, checked per frame, and that is load-bearing.** A `TOP`-stamped member violates
its chain non-deterministically; a median over the window averages that away, so the same check
written over the artifact's medians would pass on a stream it should refuse. With a per-frame
violation probability of a few percent over a ~100-frame window, the probability of zero counted
violations is below `10⁻³`.

**Reported once per process** (`RatePolicy::Once`). A chain that breaks on one frame usually breaks
on many, and the magnitude belongs in the artifact rather than in a flood of identical records.

## How to fix

- **Check the zone's BEGIN stage first** (`zone_begin_stage` in `gpu_zone.rs`). A member of a
  consecutive-partition run must open at `BOTTOM_OF_PIPE`; `TOP_OF_PIPE` is legal only for a
  bracket preceded by work that is not being attributed to it.
- **Check whether the bracket moved.** A begin that migrated past a sibling's, or a member recorded
  outside the run bracket that is supposed to contain it, produces the same report.
- **Read `[order]` beside the row.** `violations` is meaningless without `frames_checked`:
  `violations = 0` over `frames_checked = 0` is not a pass, it is a window in which nothing was
  checked — usually a family that declared no chain.
- **Do not compare the affected rows meanwhile.** Overlapping brackets make sums and differences
  over them arithmetic about two intervals that share time.
