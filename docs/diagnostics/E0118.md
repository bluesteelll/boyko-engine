# boyko-E0118 - the panic hook could not flush

## What happened

A panic ran the hook, and the hook could not claim the drain role:

```text
boyko-E0118: the panic hook could not claim the drain role; the crash file is short by 1 pass
```

The crash file exists and is missing the records that were still in the ring.

## Why the hook does not wait

The drain role is held by whoever is draining - a sink thread, or a host draining by hand. A panic
hook that **waited** for it would convert a crash into a hang, and a hang leaves no artifact at all.
So the claim is `try_claim`, and a refusal is reported rather than retried.

This is the honest trade: a short file that says it is short beats either a hung process or a file
that is quietly missing its last records. A reader who sees this code knows the tail is absent; a
reader who does not see it can trust that what is there is everything.

`Every`, not `Once`: two threads panicking are two facts, and a latch would report one of them.

## How to fix

- **Nothing, at the moment it fires.** This is a report about a crash already in progress.
- **For the next run**: a host that drains by hand holds the role for the duration of its pass.
  Shorter passes shrink the window. `SinkMode::Thread` moves draining to a dedicated thread that
  releases the role between passes.
- **The records are not lost from the ring** - they are simply unwritten. A process that survives
  the panic (a caught unwind, a worker thread dying) drains them on its next pass.
