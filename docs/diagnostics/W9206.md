# boyko-W9206 - a contrast could not be resolved

## What happened

Two measured legs were compared and the comparison produced no verdict:

```text
boyko-W9206: a contrast could not be resolved (floor_workload_mismatch); the comparison carries no
verdict
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_app/src/profiling/contrast.rs` (`resolve`). The `Contrast::NotResolved`
value still carries the delta and the band, so a caller that wants the raw numbers has them; what
it does not have is a direction.

## Why

`resolve` refuses rather than guesses. The reason is an **argument**, not part of the message,
because the five refusals need different things from a reader:

| reason | what it means |
|---|---|
| `floor_workload_mismatch` | the decidability floor was measured on a different configuration, so it bounds nothing here |
| `twin_workload_mismatch` | so was the null control |
| `window_incomplete` | a leg's window dropped samples, so its median is over an unknown subset (see `boyko-W9205`) |
| `epoch_break` | the legs came from different processes, so their clocks share no origin |
| `label_not_measured` | a subscribed zone came back `Lost`, `Torn` or `NotBracketed` |

**`below_band` is NOT reported, and that is the decision worth knowing.** It is the sixth
`NotResolvedReason` and the enum documents it in its own words as *"the common case, and not an
error"*: the comparison **was** made and the difference is inside the band. Warning on it would put
a `Warn` in the log of every run that correctly found no change.

**A direct call rather than a raised flag.** `resolve` runs after a measurement, usually after the
frame loop has stopped, and the profiler's fold is the only consumer of the sticky-bit word — a bit
raised here would wait for a fold that never comes.

## How to fix

- **`*_workload_mismatch`: re-measure the floor or the twin on the configuration you are
  comparing.** The tag is derived from the whole boot-resolved render path, so it changes when the
  path does; a floor from a Deferred run bounds nothing about a VB one.
- **`window_incomplete` / `label_not_measured`: fix the measurement, not the comparison.** Both mean
  a leg's numbers are over data the run did not fully collect.
- **`epoch_break`: compare legs from one process.** Two processes' GPU clocks have no shared origin,
  and no amount of arithmetic recovers one.
