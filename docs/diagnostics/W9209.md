# boyko-W9209 — late profiling samples were dropped

## What happened

Samples reached the fold carrying a `stamp` older than the oldest frame the window still retains:

```text
boyko-W9209: 3 profiling samples arrived after their frame had left the retained window
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_ecs/src/ecs/core/profiling/diag.rs` (`report_late`).

The window is `WINDOW = 121` frames — about 2.02 s at 60 Hz. A sample whose frame has rotated out
has nowhere to go.

## Why they are dropped rather than attributed to the oldest frame

Putting a late sample in the floor frame would place a measurement in a frame it did not happen in.
That is a **wrong number**, and a wrong number in a profile is worse than a missing one because it
survives every downstream check: it has a plausible magnitude, it sits in a frame that exists, and
nothing later can tell it apart from a real one.

## What usually causes it

- **A span held open across more than two seconds.** A `Span` stamps at OPEN and is written at
  CLOSE, so a bracket around a long load, a shader compile or a blocking I/O call is stamped where
  it began and arrives 121 frames later.
- **A thread that emits and then stalls.** Its samples sit in its lane region until it produces
  again or the fold reaches it; a worker parked for two seconds delivers late.
- **A fold that stopped running.** If a host stops calling `App::update_with_delta`, nothing drains,
  and everything in the rings is late by the time it resumes.

## Why it is a warning and not an error

The window is intact and every surviving figure is exact. What is true is that the window is a
lower bound, and `drops.late` carries the magnitude. A contrast over a window with any drop refuses
to resolve.

## Why it fires once

`RatePolicy::Once`. A long-open bracket produces the condition on every fold for as long as it
lives; the code says that it happened and the counter keeps the count.

## How to fix

- **Do not bracket work longer than the window with a `Span`.** A load, a compile or a level
  transition wants a counter or a gauge, not an interval the profiler has to hold open across
  two seconds of frames.
- **Fold every frame.** The fold is at the top of `App::update_with_delta`; a host that skips it
  during loading will be told about every sample produced in the meantime.
