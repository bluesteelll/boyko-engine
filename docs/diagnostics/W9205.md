# boyko-W9205 - zones were lost in this window

## What happened

A measurement window folded to its rows with pairs missing:

```text
boyko-W9205: zones were lost in this window: 7 lost, 2 torn, 51 measured -- the figures are folded
from fewer samples than the window ran
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_app/src/profiling/reduce.rs` (`WindowReducer::finish`). The rows are
still written and the artifact is still valid; each row's `n` is smaller than the window's frame
count.

## Why

`lost` means the recorder **bracketed** a pair and its results never came back. `torn` means the
begin landed and the end did not. Both are different from `not_bracketed`, which says a leg does
not run that pass at all — a stated absence, not a loss — and the label census carries the three
apart for exactly this reason.

**Reported at `finish`, not at the increment.** The claim is about the *window*: one record
carrying the window's totals is what a reader acts on, where a report per lost pair would be a
storm describing itself.

**A direct call rather than a raised flag.** The profiler's fold is the single consumer of the
sticky-bit word, and a window that finishes at the end of a measured run is finishing after the
last fold — a bit raised there is a report nobody takes.

## How to fix

- **Read it as a caveat on the medians, not as a failure.** A window that measured 51 of 60 pairs
  produced real numbers over a smaller sample; the standard-error term in a contrast's band already
  widens for it.
- **`lost` climbing steadily** usually means the ring is shallower than the GPU's latency: a slot is
  reclaimed before its results are ready. `GPU_RING_DEPTH` against frames-in-flight is the ratio to
  check.
- **`torn` is a different animal** — a begin without an end means a bracket opened on a path that
  returned early. Look for a pass that early-outs between `begin` and `end`.
- **If the whole window is lost**, the device's timestamps are probably unusable and the run should
  have been refused at boot — see `boyko-W3008`.
