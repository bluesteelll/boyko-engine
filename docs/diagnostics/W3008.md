# boyko-W3008 - a profiling knob the device cannot serve

## What happened

`BOYKO_VB_ZONE` asked for the GPU zone recorder on a device whose timestamps are unusable:

```text
boyko-W3008: BOYKO_VB_ZONE is set but this device's timestamps are unusable - the zone recorder
stays disabled
```

Raised from `crates/boyko_app/src/gpu_scene/mod.rs`, during `GpuSceneBundles::boot`. The run
proceeds with no GPU timing instrument.

## Why

`DeviceCaps::timestamps_usable()` is the conjunction of the two facts a timestamp needs to mean
anything: the queue family must support timestamps at all, and `timestampPeriod` must be a value
that converts ticks to nanoseconds. A device failing either would produce query results that
*parse* and are *meaningless*, which is worse than none.

**Why this is a warning and not a silent skip.** A measurement run whose instrument silently did
not arm produces an artifact with an empty zone table, and the reader's natural conclusion is
"the zones did not execute" rather than "the clock was never readable". The whole profiling half of
this campaign exists because that class of confusion is expensive; this record is one line of
prevention.

`RatePolicy::Once`, per site. `boot` runs once per process.

## How to fix

- **Check the queue family.** `VK_QUEUE_GRAPHICS_BIT` implies timestamp support on every device
  this engine targets; a compute-only or transfer queue does not. If the host selected an unusual
  family, that selection is the bug.
- **Check `timestampPeriod`.** A driver reporting `0` cannot be converted from; this is rare and is
  a driver bug rather than a configuration.
- **Unset `BOYKO_VB_ZONE`** if you do not need GPU timings - the CPU-side profiler
  (`BOYKO_PROFILE_ON`) is independent of this capability and works on every device.
- **Related but different:** if timestamps *are* usable and the artifact is still empty, the
  condition is `boyko-W9202` or `boyko-W9203`, not this one.
