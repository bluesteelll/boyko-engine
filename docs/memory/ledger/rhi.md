## Findings

- 14 of the 64 rows need no storage at all after the rewrite: the cold spill paths get chunked over existing inline arrays, brick_atlas and mesh_sdf_texture bake straight into mapped memory, and the probes stop allocating.
- `ScratchColumn` is already usable from another crate: `new` and `build_view` are pub, and so are the build view's push, clear and truncate. What actually blocks this group is the crate edge plus the id budget. `register_new` mints a FRESH id on every call, so the 19 FrameGraph lanes would use up 19 of the 512 ids without KF-01.
