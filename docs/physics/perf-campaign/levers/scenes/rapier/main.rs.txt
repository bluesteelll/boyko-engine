//! rapier-parity: Rapier 0.36 on boyko's J-T pyramid, the second parity comparator after Jolt 5.6.
//!
//! **This is the `dyn/` copy** (boyko lane DYN-SCENES): the frozen harness `../src/main.rs` (FROZEN-9b
//! §1, sha256 `a9269981…`) plus the dynamic parity scenes, built into its own exes
//! (`dyn/bin/rapier-parity-dyn-<arm>.exe`) so that no frozen file, exe or pin moves. Every J-T row
//! runs here as on the frozen exes (gate 2 of the lane replays all 70 frozen rows at W1 and W8
//! against their fixtures); the additions:
//!
//! * `--dyn kick|shoot|slide`: a dynamic program on J-T (`src/spec.rs`, a byte copy of boyko's
//!   `benches/jolt_parity_pyramid/dyn_spec.rs`; the glue is `src/dyn_scenes.rs`). Events are
//!   applied between steps, before the step's `Instant::now()`; `--steps` defaults to the program's
//!   length. Projectiles carry the row's gyroscopic setting and cannot sleep, like every J-T box.
//! * `--sanity PATH`: the shared sanity CSV (scored by boyko's `scenes/gate/dyn_sanity.py`) and the
//!   static-pair receipts in the summary's `dyn` object. `--scene-dump PATH`: the canonical scene
//!   dump, read back from the sets. Both structural: never in a timed row.
//! * Scene identity (g1) expects the program's statics (1 for J-T, 6 for S-SHOOT / S-SLIDE), and the
//!   `--receipt` sleep gate expects every body of the step awake (1240 for J-T, up to 1300 for
//!   S-SHOOT): the J-T form of both is unchanged (critique W5).
//! * The SUMMARY gains `source_fnv1a64.spec_rs` / `dyn_scenes_rs` and, only for a dynamic run, a
//!   `dyn` object; `RUNNER_ID` is the frozen one, so a J-T row's SUMMARY equals the frozen exe's but
//!   for the source hashes, the exe and the timing keys. The exe's sha256 names it.
//!
//! An EXTERNAL benchmark comparator (not a boyko workspace member, its own `Cargo.lock`), shaped
//! after boyko's fixed-window runner `crates/boyko_physics/benches/jolt_parity_pyramid.rs`: one
//! world per process, stepped `--steps` times from t = 0 with no warm-up, one `Instant` pair
//! around each `PhysicsPipeline::step` and nothing else inside it. Everything else the runner reads
//! (receipts, pose, pool size) happens after the pair closes.
//!
//! # The scene (J-T)
//!
//! Jolt 5.6 `PerformanceTest/PyramidScene.h` and boyko's `spawn_scene` (`--scene jolt`), index for
//! index: a fixed floor cuboid of half-extents (50, 1, 50) at (0, −1, 0); 15 layers `i`, and for
//! `j, k` in `[i/2, 15 − (i+1)/2)` a cuboid of half-extent 1 (no convex radius, no contact skin) at
//! `(−15 + 2j + odd, 1 + 2.5 i, −15 + 2k + odd)`, `odd = 1` on odd layers: 1240 dynamic bodies in
//! boyko's spawn order. Identity rotation, zero velocity, zero damping (boyko has none; Jolt's is
//! removed by the parity patch), density 1 (Rapier's default: m = 8, the inverse mass 0.125 and
//! inverse inertia 0.1875 boyko's runner writes), friction 0.2 and restitution 0 on every collider.
//! Gravity (0, −9.81, 0), dt = 1/60, one step per frame, sleeping off (`can_sleep(false)` on every
//! box), CCD off (`max_ccd_substeps = 0`, which disables Rapier's automatic fixed-collider CCD too).
//!
//! Friction combine: every collider is `CoefficientCombineRule::Max`, boyko's rule, so the effective
//! coefficient is max(0.2, 0.2) = 0.2. Jolt's √(0.2·0.2) and Rapier's default Average (0.2+0.2)/2
//! give 0.2 as well; the summary carries the combined value Rapier computes and, under `--receipt`,
//! the min/max `friction` of the final step's solver manifolds.
//!
//! # Configurations (`--cfg`)
//!
//! * `rapier-default`: `IntegrationParameters::default()` plus the scene invariants (dt, CCD off);
//!   body defaults kept, gyroscopic forces included. Rows per step = 8·Np + 12·Nm.
//! * `matched`: the closest match to boyko's solver: 4 substeps × (1 biased pass + 2 relax passes),
//!   friction in the biased pass, Coulomb friction (2 rows per point), 30 Hz / ζ 10 softness on
//!   every contact (the fixed floor too; Rapier's default stiffens it to 60 Hz), bias cap 4 m/s,
//!   speculative distance 0.02 (Jolt's, and boyko's V2), contact recycling at 1 mm (boyko's reuse
//!   τ and Jolt's cache), gyroscopic forces off. Rows per step = 36·Np, boyko's formula.
//!
//! Rows are solver row-iterations per step, the equal-work unit of the Jolt dossier, from Rapier's
//! own loop (`staged_island_solver/worker.rs`, `solve.rs:98`): `S·[P·(Np + fib·F) + Q·(Np + F)]`
//! with `F = 3·Nm` (Simplified) or `2·Np` (Coulomb), warm start not counted.
//!
//! # Builds (the SIMD arm)
//!
//! Exactly one of three cargo features names the build, and `compile_error!` refuses any other
//! combination. rapier3d 0.36 has no `simd4` feature: its SIMD is always on through `wide`, at 4
//! lanes, and its `simd8` feature widens that to 8.
//!
//! * `simd8` (default): `rapier3d/simd8` (and `parry3d/simd8`), 8-lane AVX2. Built by
//!   `cargo build --release --locked`.
//! * `simd4`: a marker feature that enables nothing in rapier3d, so Rapier runs at its 4-lane
//!   default. Built by `cargo build --release --locked --no-default-features --features simd4`.
//! * `det`: `rapier3d/enhanced-determinism` (4 lanes, libm). Not timed: the bitwise-determinism
//!   receipt only. It cannot be combined with `simd8` (rapier3d `lib.rs:19-21`).
//!
//! # Counters
//!
//! `PhysicsPipeline::new()` turns Rapier's statistics counters on, and with them on `init_and_solve`
//! walks every solver manifold on every step (`staged_island_solver/init.rs:601-610`), serially,
//! inside the timed pair. Our J-T runner does its counts after the pair closes. So the counters are
//! OFF unless `--receipt` is given (`--counters on|off` overrides), and the state in force is read
//! back from the pipeline and recorded as `config.counters_enabled`. Nothing in the dynamics reads
//! the counters, so the final pose does not depend on them (g2t checks it). The `--receipt` twins
//! keep them on: they are the recount gate.
//!
//! # Threads (`--workers W`, `--install`)
//!
//! A rayon pool of exactly W threads. `--install loop` (default): the whole step loop runs inside
//! one `pool.install`, so the timed pair holds only `step`, and the loop thread is the solver's
//! inline worker 0 — W threads through the parallel work, the calling thread blocked outside, as
//! boyko's W workers and Jolt's `-t=W`. `--install step`: `pool.install` per step from the main
//! thread and the pair around it — Rapier's own `configure_thread_pool` shape, which prices the
//! injection. The receipt `rayon::current_num_threads()` is read inside the install, every step in
//! loop mode (before and after the loop in step mode), and must equal W.
//!
//! # Flags
//!
//! ```text
//! --cfg rapier-default|matched   (default rapier-default)
//! --gap G                        layer gap in metres, layers at 1 + (2 + G) i (default 0.5 = J-T)
//! --workers W                    rayon pool size (default 1)
//! --steps N                      steps from spawn (default 500)
//! --window A..B                  the summary's timing window, A < B <= N (default 0..N)
//! --install loop|step            where pool.install sits (default loop)
//! --receipt                      per-step counts (untimed) into the CSV and the summary
//! --counters on|off              Rapier's statistics counters (default: on iff --receipt)
//! --csv PATH                     per-step CSV: step, wall_ns, pool_threads [, receipt columns]
//! --pose-out PATH                final pose bytes (boyko's --pose-out format)
//! --spawn-pose-out PATH          the same format, written after spawn and before the first step
//! --expect-pose PATH             compare the final pose bytes with a file; exit 4 if they differ
//! --perturb B:AXIS[:DIR]         move box B's spawn coordinate AXIS (x|y|z) by one f32 ulp, DIR
//!                                +1 (default) or -1: the F0 ensemble's BOYKO_F0_PERTURB, as a flag
//! --label TEXT                   echoed into the summary
//! ```
//!
//! # Knobs (rev 3): overrides applied on top of the `--cfg` base
//!
//! Every flag below overrides one field of the base configuration (`IntegrationParameters`, a body
//! setting or a broad-phase setting) and nothing else. The full `IntegrationParameters` in force is
//! recorded in the SUMMARY `config`, and the flags that were given are echoed in `config.knobs`. A
//! run without any knob flag is byte-for-byte the rev-2 run of its `--cfg`.
//!
//! ```text
//! --substeps N          num_solver_iterations (substeps per step, >= 1)
//! --pgs N               num_internal_pgs_iterations (biased passes per substep, >= 1)
//! --stab N              num_internal_stabilization_iterations (unbiased/relax passes per substep)
//! --friction-in-bias on|off   friction_in_bias_pass (ignored by Rapier when --stab is 0)
//! --friction-model simplified|coulomb
//! --clustering on|off   contact_clustering (no effect on cuboid pairs: one manifold per pair)
//! --prediction D        normalized_prediction_distance (speculative contact distance, m)
//! --recycling on|off    contact_recycling: Rapier's analogue of boyko's `--contact-reuse`
//! --recycle-distance D  normalized_contact_recycle_distance (m; used only with --recycling on)
//! --warmstart C         warmstart_coefficient in [0, 1]
//! --max-bias V          normalized_max_corrective_velocity (m/s)
//! --contact-hz H --contact-zeta Z --static-hz H --static-zeta Z   contact / fixed-body softness
//! --max-linvel V|off    normalized_max_linear_velocity ("off" = f32::MAX: no per-substep cap)
//! --ccd-substeps N      max_ccd_substeps (0 = all CCD off, the scene invariant)
//! --gyro on|off         per-body gyroscopic forces (Rapier's body default is on)
//! --bvh-opt subtree|none        broad-phase BVH optimization strategy
//! --bvh-adaptive on|off         broad-phase adaptive change-detection margin
//! ```
//!
//! # Output
//!
//! * stdout: a few readable lines, then one line `SUMMARY {json}`.
//! * Pose bytes: every dynamic body's position, linear velocity, rotation (x, y, z, w) and angular
//!   velocity as 13 little-endian `f32`, in spawn order; the hash is FNV-1a 64 over them. Byte for
//!   byte the format of boyko's runner, so `metrics.py` and the equal-work scripts read it unchanged.
//! * Timing columns (`wall_ns`, `window_mean_ns`) exist and are NOT a verdict unless the run was
//!   taken on a quiet machine.
//! * The CSV always carries `pool_threads` (the rayon pool size read after each step, inside the
//!   install, `--install loop`; 0 = not read, `--install step`), with or without `--receipt`.
//! * Exit code: 0 ok; 2 bad flags or an unwritable file; 3 void (a receipt gate failed: scene
//!   identity, pool size, the loop not on a pool worker, a sleeping body, a counter disagreeing
//!   with the recount); 4 `--expect-pose` mismatch.

// ── Scene constants (Jolt `PyramidScene.h` / boyko `jolt_parity_pyramid.rs`) ─────────────────────

/// `cBoxSize`: the pitch between neighbouring boxes in a layer.
const BOX_SIZE: f32 = 2.0;
/// `cHalfBoxSize`: every box's half-extent on every axis.
const HALF_BOX: f32 = 0.5 * BOX_SIZE;
/// `cBoxSeparation`: the J-T layer gap, and the default of `--gap`.
const BOX_SEPARATION: f32 = 0.5;
/// `cPyramidHeight`: the number of layers.
const PYRAMID_HEIGHT: i32 = 15;
/// Dynamic bodies in the pyramid.
const PYRAMID_BODIES: usize = 1240;
/// The floor's half-extents (`BoxShape(Vec3(50, 1, 50))`).
const FLOOR_HALF_EXTENTS: [f32; 3] = [50.0, 1.0, 50.0];
/// The floor's centre.
const FLOOR_CENTER: [f32; 3] = [0.0, -1.0, 0.0];
/// Jolt's default friction, boyko's `JOLT_FRICTION`.
const FRICTION: f32 = 0.2;
/// Restitution on every collider.
const RESTITUTION: f32 = 0.0;
/// Rapier's default density: m = 8 for a cube of half-extent 1, boyko's unit-density box.
const DENSITY: f32 = 1.0;
/// Jolt's `cDeltaTime`, boyko's `DT`.
const DT: f32 = 1.0 / 60.0;
/// Gravity along y.
const GRAVITY_Y: f32 = -9.81;
/// boyko's `BOX_INV_MASS`.
const OURS_BOX_INV_MASS: f32 = 0.125;
/// boyko's `BOX_INV_INERTIA`.
const OURS_BOX_INV_INERTIA: f32 = 0.1875;
/// boyko's first spawned box (i = j = k = 0).
const OURS_FIRST: [f32; 3] = [-15.0, 1.0, -15.0];
/// boyko's last spawned box, the top box (i = 14, j = k = 7).
const OURS_LAST: [f32; 3] = [-1.0, 36.0, -1.0];
/// The spawned pile's AABB (box extents included): layer 0 spans x, z in [−16, 14], y from 0; the
/// top box reaches y = 37.
const SPAWN_AABB_MIN: [f32; 3] = [-16.0, 0.0, -16.0];
/// See [`SPAWN_AABB_MIN`].
const SPAWN_AABB_MAX: [f32; 3] = [14.0, 37.0, 14.0];

/// The top box's spawn position at layer gap `gap`: boyko's expression for layer 14, j = k = 7. At
/// the J-T gap it is the literal [`OURS_LAST`], so that default stays independently pinned.
fn expected_last(gap: f32) -> [f32; 3] {
    if gap == BOX_SEPARATION {
        OURS_LAST
    } else {
        [-1.0, 1.0 + (BOX_SIZE + gap) * (PYRAMID_HEIGHT - 1) as f32, -1.0]
    }
}

/// The spawned pile's AABB at layer gap `gap` (box extents included); the literal pair at the J-T gap.
fn expected_aabb(gap: f32) -> ([f32; 3], [f32; 3]) {
    if gap == BOX_SEPARATION {
        (SPAWN_AABB_MIN, SPAWN_AABB_MAX)
    } else {
        (
            SPAWN_AABB_MIN,
            [
                SPAWN_AABB_MAX[0],
                expected_last(gap)[1] + HALF_BOX,
                SPAWN_AABB_MAX[2],
            ],
        )
    }
}

// ── Command line ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/// The solver configuration a run sets.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Debug)]
enum CfgKind {
    /// `IntegrationParameters::default()` plus the scene invariants.
    RapierDefault,
    /// The closest match to boyko's solver.
    Matched,
}

impl CfgKind {
    fn name(self) -> &'static str {
        match self {
            Self::RapierDefault => "rapier-default",
            Self::Matched => "matched",
        }
    }
}

/// Where `pool.install` sits.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Debug)]
enum InstallMode {
    /// One install around the whole step loop.
    Loop,
    /// One install per step.
    Step,
}

impl InstallMode {
    fn name(self) -> &'static str {
        match self {
            Self::Loop => "loop",
            Self::Step => "step",
        }
    }
}

/// A one-ulp move of one box's spawn coordinate (the F0 ensemble's `BOYKO_F0_PERTURB`).
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug)]
struct Perturb {
    body: usize,
    axis: usize,
    dir: i32,
}

/// Overrides of single configuration fields, applied on top of the `--cfg` base. `None` leaves the
/// base value alone.
#[derive(Clone, Default)]
struct Knobs {
    substeps: Option<usize>,
    pgs: Option<usize>,
    stab: Option<usize>,
    friction_in_bias: Option<bool>,
    friction_model: Option<FrictionModel>,
    clustering: Option<bool>,
    prediction: Option<f32>,
    recycling: Option<bool>,
    recycle_distance: Option<f32>,
    warmstart: Option<f32>,
    max_bias: Option<f32>,
    contact_hz: Option<f32>,
    contact_zeta: Option<f32>,
    static_hz: Option<f32>,
    static_zeta: Option<f32>,
    /// `Some(None)` = `--max-linvel off`.
    max_linvel: Option<Option<f32>>,
    ccd_substeps: Option<usize>,
    gyro: Option<bool>,
    bvh_opt: Option<BvhOptimizationStrategy>,
    bvh_adaptive: Option<bool>,
    /// The flags given, in command-line order, as `--flag=value`, echoed into `config.knobs`.
    given: Vec<String>,
}

/// The parsed command line.
struct Args {
    cfg: CfgKind,
    knobs: Knobs,
    /// `--gap`: the layer gap, layers at `1 + (BOX_SIZE + gap) i`.
    gap: f32,
    workers: usize,
    steps: usize,
    window: (usize, usize),
    install: InstallMode,
    receipt: bool,
    /// `--counters`; `None` = on iff `receipt`.
    counters: Option<bool>,
    csv: Option<PathBuf>,
    pose_out: Option<PathBuf>,
    spawn_pose_out: Option<PathBuf>,
    expect_pose: Option<PathBuf>,
    perturb: Option<Perturb>,
    label: Option<String>,
    /// `--dyn`: the dynamic program; `None` is J-T.
    dyn_program: Option<Program>,
    /// `--sanity PATH`.
    sanity: Option<PathBuf>,
    /// `--scene-dump PATH`.
    scene_dump: Option<PathBuf>,
    raw: Vec<String>,
}

impl Args {
    /// Whether the run reads a dynamic program (`--dyn`, `--sanity` or `--scene-dump`).
    fn dyn_any(&self) -> bool {
        self.dyn_program.is_some() || self.sanity.is_some() || self.scene_dump.is_some()
    }

    /// Whether Rapier's statistics counters run: on for a receipt run (they are the recount gate),
    /// off for a timed run, unless `--counters` says otherwise.
    fn counters_enabled(&self) -> bool {
        self.counters.unwrap_or(self.receipt)
    }
}

fn parse_num<T: std::str::FromStr>(flag: &str, v: Option<String>) -> Result<T, String> {
    let v = v.ok_or_else(|| format!("{flag} needs a value"))?;
    v.parse::<T>().map_err(|_| format!("{flag}: cannot parse {v:?}"))
}

fn parse_onoff(flag: &str, v: Option<String>) -> Result<bool, String> {
    match v.as_deref() {
        Some("on") => Ok(true),
        Some("off") => Ok(false),
        other => Err(format!("{flag}: expected on|off, got {other:?}")),
    }
}

/// A finite `f32` that is `>= 0`.
fn parse_nonneg(flag: &str, v: Option<String>) -> Result<f32, String> {
    let x: f32 = parse_num(flag, v)?;
    if x.is_finite() && x >= 0.0 {
        Ok(x)
    } else {
        Err(format!("{flag} {x} must be finite and >= 0"))
    }
}

/// Parses the knob flag `flag` into `k`; `Ok(false)` when `flag` is not a knob.
fn parse_knob(k: &mut Knobs, flag: &str, it: &mut impl Iterator<Item = String>) -> Result<bool, String> {
    let raw = match flag {
        "--substeps" | "--pgs" | "--stab" | "--friction-in-bias" | "--friction-model" | "--clustering"
        | "--prediction" | "--recycling" | "--recycle-distance" | "--warmstart" | "--max-bias"
        | "--contact-hz" | "--contact-zeta" | "--static-hz" | "--static-zeta" | "--max-linvel"
        | "--ccd-substeps" | "--gyro" | "--bvh-opt" | "--bvh-adaptive" => it.next(),
        _ => return Ok(false),
    };
    if let Some(v) = &raw {
        k.given.push(format!("{flag}={v}"));
    }
    match flag {
        "--substeps" => {
            let n: usize = parse_num(flag, raw)?;
            if n == 0 {
                return Err("--substeps must be >= 1".to_owned());
            }
            k.substeps = Some(n);
        }
        "--pgs" => {
            let n: usize = parse_num(flag, raw)?;
            if n == 0 {
                return Err(
                    "--pgs must be >= 1 (a substep without a biased pass integrates nothing)".to_owned(),
                );
            }
            k.pgs = Some(n);
        }
        "--stab" => k.stab = Some(parse_num(flag, raw)?),
        "--friction-in-bias" => k.friction_in_bias = Some(parse_onoff(flag, raw)?),
        "--friction-model" => {
            k.friction_model = Some(match raw.as_deref() {
                Some("simplified") => FrictionModel::Simplified,
                Some("coulomb") => FrictionModel::Coulomb,
                other => return Err(format!("{flag}: expected simplified|coulomb, got {other:?}")),
            })
        }
        "--clustering" => k.clustering = Some(parse_onoff(flag, raw)?),
        "--prediction" => k.prediction = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--recycling" => k.recycling = Some(parse_onoff(flag, raw)?),
        "--recycle-distance" => k.recycle_distance = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--warmstart" => {
            let c = parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?;
            if c > 1.0 {
                return Err(format!("{flag} {c} must be in [0, 1]"));
            }
            k.warmstart = Some(c);
        }
        "--max-bias" => k.max_bias = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--contact-hz" => k.contact_hz = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--contact-zeta" => k.contact_zeta = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--static-hz" => k.static_hz = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--static-zeta" => k.static_zeta = Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?),
        "--max-linvel" => {
            k.max_linvel = Some(if raw.as_deref() == Some("off") {
                None
            } else {
                Some(parse_nonneg(flag, raw)?)
            })
        }
        "--ccd-substeps" => k.ccd_substeps = Some(parse_num(flag, raw)?),
        "--gyro" => k.gyro = Some(parse_onoff(flag, raw)?),
        "--bvh-opt" => {
            k.bvh_opt = Some(match raw.as_deref() {
                Some("subtree") => BvhOptimizationStrategy::SubtreeOptimizer,
                Some("none") => BvhOptimizationStrategy::None,
                other => return Err(format!("{flag}: expected subtree|none, got {other:?}")),
            })
        }
        "--bvh-adaptive" => k.bvh_adaptive = Some(parse_onoff(flag, raw)?),
        _ => unreachable!("invariant: every knob flag of the first match has an arm here"),
    }
    Ok(true)
}

fn parse_path(flag: &str, v: Option<String>) -> Result<PathBuf, String> {
    v.map(PathBuf::from).ok_or_else(|| format!("{flag} needs a path"))
}

fn parse_perturb(v: Option<String>) -> Result<Perturb, String> {
    let v = v.ok_or("--perturb needs B:AXIS[:DIR]")?;
    let mut it = v.split(':');
    let body = it
        .next()
        .and_then(|s| s.trim().parse::<usize>().ok())
        .ok_or_else(|| format!("--perturb {v:?}: bad box index"))?;
    let axis = match it.next().map(str::trim) {
        Some("x") => 0,
        Some("y") => 1,
        Some("z") => 2,
        other => return Err(format!("--perturb {v:?}: bad axis {other:?}")),
    };
    let dir = match it.next().map(str::trim) {
        None | Some("+1" | "1") => 1,
        Some("-1") => -1,
        Some(other) => return Err(format!("--perturb {v:?}: bad direction {other:?}")),
    };
    if it.next().is_some() {
        return Err(format!("--perturb {v:?}: too many fields"));
    }
    if body >= PYRAMID_BODIES {
        return Err(format!(
            "--perturb {v:?}: box {body} out of range 0..{PYRAMID_BODIES}"
        ));
    }
    Ok(Perturb { body, axis, dir })
}

fn parse_args() -> Result<Args, String> {
    let raw: Vec<String> = std::env::args().skip(1).collect();
    let mut it = raw.clone().into_iter();
    let mut cfg = CfgKind::RapierDefault;
    let mut knobs = Knobs::default();
    let mut gap = BOX_SEPARATION;
    let mut workers = 1usize;
    let mut steps = None;
    let mut window = None;
    let mut install = InstallMode::Loop;
    let mut receipt = false;
    let mut counters = None;
    let mut csv = None;
    let mut pose_out = None;
    let mut spawn_pose_out = None;
    let mut expect_pose = None;
    let mut perturb = None;
    let mut label = None;
    let mut dyn_program = None;
    let mut sanity = None;
    let mut scene_dump = None;
    while let Some(flag) = it.next() {
        match flag.as_str() {
            "--cfg" => {
                cfg = match it.next().as_deref() {
                    Some("rapier-default") => CfgKind::RapierDefault,
                    Some("matched") => CfgKind::Matched,
                    other => return Err(format!("--cfg: expected rapier-default|matched, got {other:?}")),
                }
            }
            "--gap" => {
                gap = parse_num("--gap", it.next())?;
                if !gap.is_finite() || gap < 0.0 {
                    return Err(format!("--gap {gap} must be finite and >= 0"));
                }
            }
            "--workers" => workers = parse_num("--workers", it.next())?,
            "--steps" => steps = Some(parse_num("--steps", it.next())?),
            "--window" => {
                let v = it.next().ok_or("--window needs A..B")?;
                let (a, b) = v
                    .split_once("..")
                    .ok_or_else(|| format!("--window {v:?}: expected A..B"))?;
                window = Some((
                    parse_num::<usize>("--window", Some(a.to_owned()))?,
                    parse_num::<usize>("--window", Some(b.to_owned()))?,
                ));
            }
            "--install" => {
                install = match it.next().as_deref() {
                    Some("loop") => InstallMode::Loop,
                    Some("step") => InstallMode::Step,
                    other => return Err(format!("--install: expected loop|step, got {other:?}")),
                }
            }
            "--receipt" => receipt = true,
            "--counters" => {
                counters = Some(match it.next().as_deref() {
                    Some("on") => true,
                    Some("off") => false,
                    other => return Err(format!("--counters: expected on|off, got {other:?}")),
                })
            }
            "--csv" => csv = Some(parse_path("--csv", it.next())?),
            "--pose-out" => pose_out = Some(parse_path("--pose-out", it.next())?),
            "--spawn-pose-out" => spawn_pose_out = Some(parse_path("--spawn-pose-out", it.next())?),
            "--expect-pose" => expect_pose = Some(parse_path("--expect-pose", it.next())?),
            "--perturb" => perturb = Some(parse_perturb(it.next())?),
            "--label" => label = Some(it.next().ok_or("--label needs a value")?),
            "--dyn" => {
                let v = it.next().ok_or("--dyn needs a program")?;
                dyn_program = Some(match Program::parse(&v) {
                    Some(Program::None) | None => {
                        return Err(format!("--dyn: expected kick|shoot|slide, got {v:?} (S-LAND is the J-T row itself)"));
                    }
                    Some(p) => p,
                });
            }
            "--sanity" => sanity = Some(parse_path("--sanity", it.next())?),
            "--scene-dump" => scene_dump = Some(parse_path("--scene-dump", it.next())?),
            "-h" | "--help" => return Err(USAGE.to_owned()),
            other => {
                if !parse_knob(&mut knobs, other, &mut it)? {
                    return Err(format!("unknown flag {other:?}\n{USAGE}"));
                }
            }
        }
    }
    if workers == 0 {
        return Err("--workers must be >= 1".to_owned());
    }
    let program = dyn_program.unwrap_or(Program::None);
    let steps = steps.unwrap_or(program.steps());
    if dyn_program.is_some() || sanity.is_some() || scene_dump.is_some() {
        if gap != BOX_SEPARATION || perturb.is_some() {
            return Err("--dyn, --sanity and --scene-dump run on the J-T scene: the gap 0.5, no --perturb".to_owned());
        }
        if steps > program.steps() || (scene_dump.is_some() && steps != program.steps()) {
            return Err(format!(
                "--steps {steps}: the {} program has {} steps, and --scene-dump writes a complete program",
                program.name(),
                program.steps()
            ));
        }
    }
    if steps == 0 {
        return Err("--steps must be >= 1".to_owned());
    }
    let window = window.unwrap_or((0, steps));
    if window.0 >= window.1 || window.1 > steps {
        return Err(format!(
            "--window {}..{} must satisfy A < B <= {steps}",
            window.0, window.1
        ));
    }
    Ok(Args {
        cfg,
        knobs,
        gap,
        workers,
        steps,
        window,
        install,
        receipt,
        counters,
        csv,
        pose_out,
        spawn_pose_out,
        expect_pose,
        perturb,
        label,
        dyn_program,
        sanity,
        scene_dump,
        raw,
    })
}

/// The integration parameters of a configuration. Both carry the scene invariants: dt = 1/60 and
/// CCD off (`--ccd-substeps` may override the latter). `knobs` override single fields of the base.
fn integration_parameters(cfg: CfgKind, knobs: &Knobs) -> IntegrationParameters {
    let scene = IntegrationParameters {
        dt: DT,
        max_ccd_substeps: 0,
        ..IntegrationParameters::default()
    };
    let mut p = match cfg {
        CfgKind::RapierDefault => scene,
        CfgKind::Matched => IntegrationParameters {
            num_solver_iterations: 4,
            num_internal_pgs_iterations: 1,
            num_internal_stabilization_iterations: 2,
            friction_in_bias_pass: true,
            friction_model: FrictionModel::Coulomb,
            // boyko's `contact_hertz` 30 / `contact_damping` 10, on the floor contacts as well.
            contact_softness: SpringCoefficients::new(30.0, 10.0),
            static_contact_softness: SpringCoefficients::new(30.0, 10.0),
            normalized_max_corrective_velocity: 4.0,
            normalized_prediction_distance: 0.02,
            normalized_contact_recycle_distance: 0.001,
            warmstart_coefficient: 1.0,
            ..scene
        },
    };
    if let Some(v) = knobs.substeps {
        p.num_solver_iterations = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.pgs {
        p.num_internal_pgs_iterations = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.stab {
        p.num_internal_stabilization_iterations = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.friction_in_bias {
        p.friction_in_bias_pass = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.friction_model {
        p.friction_model = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.clustering {
        p.contact_clustering = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.prediction {
        p.normalized_prediction_distance = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.recycling {
        p.contact_recycling = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.recycle_distance {
        p.normalized_contact_recycle_distance = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.warmstart {
        p.warmstart_coefficient = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.max_bias {
        p.normalized_max_corrective_velocity = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.contact_hz {
        p.contact_softness.natural_frequency = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.contact_zeta {
        p.contact_softness.damping_ratio = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.static_hz {
        p.static_contact_softness.natural_frequency = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.static_zeta {
        p.static_contact_softness.damping_ratio = v;
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.max_linvel {
        p.normalized_max_linear_velocity = v.unwrap_or(f32::MAX);
    }
    if let Some(v) = knobs.ccd_substeps {
        p.max_ccd_substeps = v;
    }
    p
}

/// Whether a run keeps Rapier's per-body gyroscopic correction: the base configuration's choice
/// (`rapier-default` keeps Rapier's body default, `matched` has none, like boyko), unless `--gyro`
/// says otherwise.
fn gyroscopic(cfg: CfgKind, knobs: &Knobs) -> bool {
    knobs.gyro.unwrap_or(cfg == CfgKind::RapierDefault)
}

/// The broad-phase optimization strategy in force.
fn bvh_strategy(knobs: &Knobs) -> BvhOptimizationStrategy {
    knobs.bvh_opt.unwrap_or_default()
}

/// Whether the broad-phase uses its adaptive change-detection margin (Rapier's default: off).
fn bvh_adaptive(knobs: &Knobs) -> bool {
    knobs.bvh_adaptive.unwrap_or(false)
}

/// Solver row-iterations per step for `np` solver points in `nm` solver manifolds, by Rapier's own
/// loop: `S` substeps, each `P` biased passes then `Q` unbiased ones; friction runs in the biased
/// pass only when `friction_in_bias_pass` is set or there is no unbiased pass (`solve.rs:98`).
fn solver_rows(p: &IntegrationParameters, np: u64, nm: u64) -> u64 {
    let s = p.num_solver_iterations as u64;
    let pgs = p.num_internal_pgs_iterations as u64;
    let q = p.num_internal_stabilization_iterations as u64;
    let f = match p.friction_model {
        FrictionModel::Simplified => 3 * nm,
        FrictionModel::Coulomb => 2 * np,
    };
    let fib = if p.friction_in_bias_pass || q == 0 { f } else { 0 };
    s * (pgs * (np + fib) + q * (np + f))
}

// ── World ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/// Every structure `PhysicsPipeline::step` takes, plus the spawn order.
struct World {
    pipeline: PhysicsPipeline,
    params: IntegrationParameters,
    gravity: Vector,
    islands: IslandManager,
    broad_phase: BroadPhaseBvh,
    narrow_phase: NarrowPhase,
    bodies: RigidBodySet,
    colliders: ColliderSet,
    impulse_joints: ImpulseJointSet,
    multibody_joints: MultibodyJointSet,
    soft_bodies: SoftBodySet,
    ccd_solver: CCDSolver,
    floor: RigidBodyHandle,
    /// The dynamic bodies in spawn order; the last one is the top box (a dynamic scene's projectiles
    /// are appended after it).
    boxes: Vec<RigidBodyHandle>,
    /// A dynamic scene's statics after the floor, in creation order.
    statics: Vec<RigidBodyHandle>,
}

impl World {
    fn step(&mut self) {
        self.pipeline.step(
            self.gravity,
            &self.params,
            &mut self.islands,
            &mut self.broad_phase,
            &mut self.narrow_phase,
            &mut self.bodies,
            &mut self.colliders,
            &mut self.impulse_joints,
            &mut self.multibody_joints,
            &mut self.soft_bodies,
            &mut self.ccd_solver,
            &(),
            &(),
        );
    }
}

/// The receipt of a `--perturb`: the coordinate before and after.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Debug)]
struct PerturbReceipt {
    spec: Perturb,
    before: f32,
    after: f32,
}

/// Moves `p[spec.axis]` by one ulp. Refuses a zero coordinate, as the F0 knob does (one ulp of 0
/// is a subnormal, not a relative nudge).
fn apply_perturb(spec: Perturb, p: &mut [f32; 3]) -> Result<PerturbReceipt, String> {
    let before = p[spec.axis];
    if before == 0.0 || !before.is_finite() {
        return Err(format!(
            "--perturb: refusing to perturb a zero or non-finite coordinate ({before})"
        ));
    }
    let bits = before.to_bits();
    let after = f32::from_bits(if spec.dir > 0 { bits + 1 } else { bits - 1 });
    p[spec.axis] = after;
    Ok(PerturbReceipt { spec, before, after })
}

/// A collider of the scene's material.
fn material(builder: ColliderBuilder) -> ColliderBuilder {
    builder
        .friction(FRICTION)
        .friction_combine_rule(CoefficientCombineRule::Max)
        .restitution(RESTITUTION)
        .density(DENSITY)
}

/// Spawns the floor and the pyramid in boyko's spawn order (Jolt's placement loop).
fn spawn(
    cfg: CfgKind,
    knobs: &Knobs,
    gap: f32,
    perturb: Option<Perturb>,
    counters_enabled: bool,
) -> Result<(World, Option<PerturbReceipt>), String> {
    let mut bodies = RigidBodySet::new();
    let mut colliders = ColliderSet::new();
    let floor = bodies.insert(RigidBodyBuilder::fixed().translation(Vector::from_array(FLOOR_CENTER)));
    let [fx, fy, fz] = FLOOR_HALF_EXTENTS;
    colliders.insert_with_parent(material(ColliderBuilder::cuboid(fx, fy, fz)), floor, &mut bodies);

    let gyro = gyroscopic(cfg, knobs);
    let mut boxes = Vec::with_capacity(PYRAMID_BODIES);
    let mut receipt = None;
    for i in 0..PYRAMID_HEIGHT {
        let lo = i / 2;
        let hi = PYRAMID_HEIGHT - (i + 1) / 2;
        for j in lo..hi {
            for k in lo..hi {
                // boyko's expression, operand for operand, so the f32 bits are boyko's.
                let odd = if i & 1 != 0 { HALF_BOX } else { 0.0 };
                let mut p = [
                    -(PYRAMID_HEIGHT as f32) + BOX_SIZE * j as f32 + odd,
                    1.0 + (BOX_SIZE + gap) * i as f32,
                    -(PYRAMID_HEIGHT as f32) + BOX_SIZE * k as f32 + odd,
                ];
                if let Some(spec) = perturb
                    && spec.body == boxes.len()
                {
                    receipt = Some(apply_perturb(spec, &mut p)?);
                }
                let body = RigidBodyBuilder::dynamic()
                    .translation(Vector::from_array(p))
                    .can_sleep(false)
                    .gyroscopic_forces_enabled(gyro);
                let h = bodies.insert(body);
                colliders.insert_with_parent(
                    material(ColliderBuilder::cuboid(HALF_BOX, HALF_BOX, HALF_BOX)),
                    h,
                    &mut bodies,
                );
                boxes.push(h);
            }
        }
    }
    let mut pipeline = PhysicsPipeline::new();
    // `PhysicsPipeline::new()` starts with the counters on; see the module docs, "Counters".
    if !counters_enabled {
        pipeline.counters.disable();
    }
    let mut broad_phase = BroadPhaseBvh::with_optimization_strategy(bvh_strategy(knobs));
    broad_phase.adaptive_change_detection_margin = bvh_adaptive(knobs);
    let world = World {
        pipeline,
        params: integration_parameters(cfg, knobs),
        gravity: Vector::new(0.0, GRAVITY_Y, 0.0),
        islands: IslandManager::new(),
        broad_phase,
        narrow_phase: NarrowPhase::new(),
        bodies,
        colliders,
        impulse_joints: ImpulseJointSet::new(),
        multibody_joints: MultibodyJointSet::new(),
        soft_bodies: SoftBodySet::new(),
        ccd_solver: CCDSolver::new(),
        floor,
        boxes,
        statics: Vec::new(),
    };
    Ok((world, receipt))
}

// ── Scene identity (g1) ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/// What the spawned world is, read back from Rapier's sets.
struct Identity {
    dynamic_bodies: usize,
    fixed_bodies: usize,
    colliders: usize,
    first: [f32; 3],
    last: [f32; 3],
    aabb_min: [f32; 3],
    aabb_max: [f32; 3],
    inv_mass: f32,
    inv_inertia: [f32; 3],
    /// Every box has the first box's inverse mass and inertia, identity rotation and zero velocity.
    uniform: bool,
    spawn_hash: u64,
}

fn identity(w: &World) -> Identity {
    let dynamic_bodies = w.bodies.iter().filter(|(_, b)| b.is_dynamic()).count();
    let fixed_bodies = w.bodies.iter().filter(|(_, b)| b.is_fixed()).count();
    let pos = |h: RigidBodyHandle| w.bodies[h].translation().to_array();
    let first = w.boxes.first().map_or([f32::NAN; 3], |&h| pos(h));
    let last = w.boxes.last().map_or([f32::NAN; 3], |&h| pos(h));
    let mut aabb_min = [f32::INFINITY; 3];
    let mut aabb_max = [f32::NEG_INFINITY; 3];
    let mprops = |h: RigidBodyHandle| {
        let m = &w.bodies[h].mass_properties().local_mprops;
        (m.inv_mass, m.inv_principal_inertia.to_array())
    };
    let (inv_mass, inv_inertia) = w.boxes.first().map_or((f32::NAN, [f32::NAN; 3]), |&h| mprops(h));
    let mut uniform = true;
    for &h in &w.boxes {
        let b = &w.bodies[h];
        let p = pos(h);
        for a in 0..3 {
            aabb_min[a] = aabb_min[a].min(p[a] - HALF_BOX);
            aabb_max[a] = aabb_max[a].max(p[a] + HALF_BOX);
        }
        let (m, i) = mprops(h);
        uniform &= m.to_bits() == inv_mass.to_bits()
            && i.map(f32::to_bits) == inv_inertia.map(f32::to_bits)
            && b.rotation().to_array() == [0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 1.0]
            && b.linvel().to_array() == [0.0; 3]
            && b.angvel().to_array() == [0.0; 3];
    }
    Identity {
        dynamic_bodies,
        fixed_bodies,
        colliders: w.colliders.len(),
        first,
        last,
        aabb_min,
        aabb_max,
        inv_mass,
        inv_inertia,
        uniform,
        spawn_hash: fnv1a64(&pose_bytes(w)),
    }
}

/// The distance in ulps between two finite `f32`s of the same sign (0 when bit-equal).
fn ulps(a: f32, b: f32) -> i64 {
    i64::from(a.to_bits() as i32) - i64::from(b.to_bits() as i32)
}

// ── Receipts ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/// One step's counts, read after the step (untimed).
#[derive(Clone, Copy, Default)]
struct Counts {
    /// Contact-graph edges: the broad-phase pairs the narrow phase tracks.
    bp_pairs: u64,
    /// Pairs with at least one (raw) contact manifold.
    pairs_with_manifold: u64,
    /// Pairs with at least one solver contact (`has_any_active_contact`).
    pairs_active: u64,
    /// Raw manifolds and their points, before the solver's filter.
    raw_manifolds: u64,
    raw_points: u64,
    /// Solver manifolds with at least one solver contact (Nm) and their solver contacts (Np).
    manifolds: u64,
    points: u64,
    /// Solver contacts with `dist > 0` (speculative), and manifolds whose every point is.
    spec_points: u64,
    spec_manifolds: u64,
    /// Solver manifolds against the floor, and their points.
    floor_manifolds: u64,
    floor_points: u64,
    /// Solver manifolds whose normal is lateral (|n.y| < 0.5).
    lateral_manifolds: u64,
    /// Rapier's own counters: `counters.cd.ncontact_pairs`, `counters.solver.nconstraints` and
    /// `counters.solver.ncontacts`.
    counter_pairs: u64,
    counter_constraints: u64,
    counter_contacts: u64,
    /// Solver row-iterations ([`solver_rows`]).
    rows: u64,
    /// `IslandManager::num_active_bodies`.
    awake: u64,
    /// The top box's y.
    top_y: f32,
    /// Min/max effective friction over the solver manifolds.
    friction_min: f32,
    friction_max: f32,
}

fn count(w: &World) -> Counts {
    let mut c = Counts {
        friction_min: f32::INFINITY,
        friction_max: f32::NEG_INFINITY,
        ..Counts::default()
    };
    for pair in w.narrow_phase.contact_pairs() {
        c.bp_pairs += 1;
        let raw = pair.manifolds();
        if !raw.is_empty() {
            c.pairs_with_manifold += 1;
        }
        if pair.has_any_active_contact() {
            c.pairs_active += 1;
        }
        for m in raw {
            c.raw_manifolds += 1;
            c.raw_points += m.points.len() as u64;
        }
        for m in pair.solver_manifolds() {
            let sc = &m.data.solver_contacts;
            if sc.is_empty() {
                continue;
            }
            let n = sc.len() as u64;
            c.manifolds += 1;
            c.points += n;
            let spec = sc.iter().filter(|p| p.dist > 0.0).count() as u64;
            c.spec_points += spec;
            if spec == n {
                c.spec_manifolds += 1;
            }
            if m.data.rigid_body1 == Some(w.floor) || m.data.rigid_body2 == Some(w.floor) {
                c.floor_manifolds += 1;
                c.floor_points += n;
            }
            if m.data.normal.y.abs() < LATERAL_NORMAL_Y {
                c.lateral_manifolds += 1;
            }
            c.friction_min = c.friction_min.min(m.data.friction);
            c.friction_max = c.friction_max.max(m.data.friction);
        }
    }
    let counters = &w.pipeline.counters;
    c.counter_pairs = counters.cd.ncontact_pairs as u64;
    c.counter_constraints = counters.solver.nconstraints as u64;
    c.counter_contacts = counters.solver.ncontacts as u64;
    c.rows = solver_rows(&w.params, c.points, c.manifolds);
    c.awake = w.islands.num_active_bodies() as u64;
    c.top_y = w.boxes.last().map_or(f32::NAN, |&h| w.bodies[h].translation().y);
    c
}

/// One step's row.
#[derive(Clone, Copy)]
struct Row {
    wall_ns: u64,
    /// `rayon::current_num_threads()` read inside the install after the step (loop mode), else 0.
    pool_threads: usize,
    counts: Option<Counts>,
    /// The dynamic bodies after the step (1240 on J-T): what the `--receipt` sleep gate expects awake.
    bodies: usize,
}

/// Steps the world `args.steps` times through `step`, timing only the call. `pool_receipt` reads
/// the rayon pool size after each step (loop mode, where the loop runs inside the install). The
/// dynamic scenes' hook runs outside the pair: before it (the step's events) and after the readout.
fn drive<F: FnMut(&mut World)>(
    w: &mut World,
    args: &Args,
    pool_receipt: bool,
    mut step: F,
    dyn_run: &mut Option<dyn_scenes::DynRun>,
) -> Vec<Row> {
    let mut rows = Vec::with_capacity(args.steps);
    for i in 0..args.steps {
        if let Some(d) = dyn_run.as_mut() {
            d.before_step(i, w);
        }
        let t0 = Instant::now();
        step(w);
        let wall = t0.elapsed();
        // ── Untimed from here. ──
        rows.push(Row {
            wall_ns: u64::try_from(wall.as_nanos()).unwrap_or(u64::MAX),
            pool_threads: if pool_receipt {
                rayon::current_num_threads()
            } else {
                0
            },
            counts: args.receipt.then(|| count(w)),
            bodies: w.boxes.len(),
        });
        if let Some(d) = dyn_run.as_mut() {
            d.after_step(i, w);
        }
    }
    rows
}

// ── Pose ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

/// FNV-1a 64 over `bytes`.
fn fnv1a64(bytes: &[u8]) -> u64 {
    bytes
        .iter()
        .fold(FNV_OFFSET, |h, &b| (h ^ u64::from(b)).wrapping_mul(FNV_PRIME))
}

/// Every dynamic body's position, linear velocity, rotation (x, y, z, w) and angular velocity as
/// little-endian `f32` bits, in spawn order: boyko's `pose_bytes`.
fn pose_bytes(w: &World) -> Vec<u8> {
    let mut out = Vec::with_capacity(w.boxes.len() * POSE_BYTES_PER_BODY);
    for &h in &w.boxes {
        let b = &w.bodies[h];
        let [px, py, pz] = b.translation().to_array();
        let [vx, vy, vz] = b.linvel().to_array();
        let [qx, qy, qz, qw] = b.rotation().to_array();
        let [ax, ay, az] = b.angvel().to_array();
        for f in [px, py, pz, vx, vy, vz, qx, qy, qz, qw, ax, ay, az] {
            out.extend_from_slice(&f.to_bits().to_le_bytes());
        }
    }
    out
}

// ── Output helpers ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

fn json_str(s: &str) -> String {
    let mut out = String::with_capacity(s.len() + 2);
    out.push('"');
    for ch in s.chars() {
        match ch {
            '"' => out.push_str("\\\""),
            '\\' => out.push_str("\\\\"),
            c if (c as u32) < 0x20 => {
                let _ = write!(out, "\\u{:04x}", c as u32);
            }
            c => out.push(c),
        }
    }
    out.push('"');
    out
}

pub(crate) fn json_f64(v: f64) -> String {
    if v.is_finite() {
        format!("{v}")
    } else {
        "null".to_owned()
    }
}

fn json_f32(v: f32) -> String {
    if v.is_finite() {
        format!("{v}")
    } else {
        "null".to_owned()
    }
}

fn json_v3(v: [f32; 3]) -> String {
    format!("[{},{},{}]", json_f32(v[0]), json_f32(v[1]), json_f32(v[2]))
}

fn friction_model_name(m: FrictionModel) -> &'static str {
    match m {

        FrictionModel::Coulomb => "Coulomb",

}

fn csv_text(rows: &[Row], receipt: bool) -> String {
    let mut s = String::with_capacity(rows.len() * if receipt { 160 } else { 28 } + 256);
    s.push_str("step,wall_ns,pool_threads");
    if receipt {
        s.push_str(
            ",bp_pairs,pairs_with_manifold,pairs_active,raw_manifolds,raw_points,manifolds,\
             points,spec_points,spec_manifolds,floor_manifolds,floor_points,lateral_manifolds,\
             counter_pairs,counter_constraints,counter_contacts,rows,awake,top_y",
        );
    }
    s.push('\n');
    for (i, r) in rows.iter().enumerate() {
        let _ = write!(s, "{i},{},{}", r.wall_ns, r.pool_threads);
        if let Some(c) = &r.counts {
            let _ = write!(
                s,
                ",{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{},{}",
                c.bp_pairs,
                c.pairs_with_manifold,
                c.pairs_active,
                c.raw_manifolds,
                c.raw_points,
                c.manifolds,
                c.points,
                c.spec_points,
                c.spec_manifolds,
                c.floor_manifolds,
                c.floor_points,
                c.lateral_manifolds,
                c.counter_pairs,
                c.counter_constraints,
                c.counter_contacts,
                c.rows,
                c.awake,
                c.top_y,
            );
        }
        s.push('\n');
    }
    s
}

/// The placement receipt: in `--install loop` the step loop must run on a pool worker, or Rapier
/// sizes its solver from a pool other than the W-thread one. `--install step` calls into the pool
/// per step, so its loop is on the main thread by construction and has no such receipt.
fn placement_void(install: InstallMode, loop_on_worker: bool) -> Option<String> {
    (install == InstallMode::Loop && !loop_on_worker)
        .then(|| "placement: the --install loop step loop ran outside the rayon pool".to_owned())
}

// ── Main ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

fn main() -> ExitCode {
    let args = match parse_args() {
        Ok(a) => a,
        Err(e) => {
            eprintln!("rapier-parity: {e}");
            return ExitCode::from(EXIT_USAGE);
        }
    };
    let pool = match rayon::ThreadPoolBuilder::new()
        .num_threads(args.workers)
        .thread_name(|i| format!("rapier-parity-{i}"))
        .build()
    {
        Ok(p) => p,
        Err(e) => {
            eprintln!(
                "rapier-parity: building a {}-thread rayon pool: {e}",
                args.workers
            );
            return ExitCode::from(EXIT_USAGE);
        }
    };
    let (mut world, perturbed) = match spawn(
        args.cfg,
        &args.knobs,
        args.gap,
        args.perturb,
        args.counters_enabled(),
    ) {
        Ok(w) => w,
        Err(e) => {
            eprintln!("rapier-parity: {e}");
            return ExitCode::from(EXIT_USAGE);
        }
    };
    if let Some(r) = perturbed {
        eprintln!(
            "F0_PERTURB box={} axis={} dir={} before={:e} (0x{:08x}) after={:e} (0x{:08x}) delta={:e}",
            r.spec.body,
            r.spec.axis,
            r.spec.dir,
            r.before,
            r.before.to_bits(),
            r.after,
            r.after.to_bits(),
            f64::from(r.after) - f64::from(r.before)
        );
    }

    let mut exit = 0u8;
    let mut voids: Vec<String> = Vec::new();

    // The dynamic scene (module docs): its statics after the J-T boxes and its gravity, then the
    // untimed readouts; nothing at all on a J-T row.
    let program = args.dyn_program.unwrap_or(Program::None);
    if args.dyn_program.is_some() {
        dyn_scenes::configure_world(&mut world, program);
    }
    let mut dyn_run = args.dyn_any().then(|| {
        dyn_scenes::DynRun::new(
            &mut world,
            program,
            args.dyn_program.is_some(),
            gyroscopic(args.cfg, &args.knobs),
            args.steps,
            args.sanity.is_some(),
            args.scene_dump.is_some(),
        )
    });

    // g1: scene identity, on the spawned world before any step.
    let id = identity(&world);
    let perturbed_body = perturbed.map(|r| r.spec.body);
    let first_ok = perturbed_body == Some(0) || id.first == OURS_FIRST;
    let want_last = expected_last(args.gap);
    let (want_aabb_min, want_aabb_max) = expected_aabb(args.gap);
    let last_ok = perturbed_body == Some(PYRAMID_BODIES - 1) || id.last == want_last;
    let aabb_ok = perturbed.is_some() || (id.aabb_min == want_aabb_min && id.aabb_max == want_aabb_max);
    let inv_mass_ulps = ulps(id.inv_mass, OURS_BOX_INV_MASS);
    let inv_inertia_ulps = id.inv_inertia.map(|v| ulps(v, OURS_BOX_INV_INERTIA));
    // The program's statics: the floor alone on J-T (critique W5: the J-T form is the frozen one).
    let want_fixed = program.statics().len();
    if id.dynamic_bodies != PYRAMID_BODIES || world.boxes.len() != PYRAMID_BODIES || id.fixed_bodies != want_fixed {
        voids.push(format!(
            "scene: {} dynamic / {} fixed bodies, {} boxes, want {PYRAMID_BODIES} / {want_fixed}",
            id.dynamic_bodies,
            id.fixed_bodies,
            world.boxes.len()
        ));
    }
    if !first_ok || !last_ok || !aabb_ok || !id.uniform {
        voids.push(format!(
            "scene: first {:?} last {:?} aabb {:?}..{:?} uniform {}",
            id.first, id.last, id.aabb_min, id.aabb_max, id.uniform
        ));
    }
    if let Some(path) = &args.spawn_pose_out
        && let Err(e) = std::fs::write(path, pose_bytes(&world))
    {
        eprintln!("rapier-parity: writing {}: {e}", path.display());
        exit = EXIT_USAGE;
    }

    // The run.
    let (rows, install_threads, loop_on_worker) = match args.install {
        InstallMode::Loop => pool.install(|| {
            let n = rayon::current_num_threads();
            let on_worker = rayon::current_thread_index().is_some();
            let rows = drive(&mut world, &args, true, World::step, &mut dyn_run);
            (rows, [n, rayon::current_num_threads()], on_worker)
        }),
        InstallMode::Step => {
            let before = pool.install(rayon::current_num_threads);
            let rows = drive(&mut world, &args, false, |w| pool.install(|| w.step()), &mut dyn_run);
            (rows, [before, pool.install(rayon::current_num_threads)], false)
        }
    };
    drop(pool);

    // Receipt gates.
    let per_step_threads = rows.iter().map(|r| r.pool_threads).filter(|&n| n != 0);
    let pool_min = per_step_threads.clone().chain(install_threads).min().unwrap_or(0);
    let pool_max = per_step_threads.chain(install_threads).max().unwrap_or(0);
    if pool_min != args.workers || pool_max != args.workers {
        voids.push(format!(
            "threads: rayon pool read {pool_min}..{pool_max} threads, want {}",
            args.workers
        ));
    }
    voids.extend(placement_void(args.install, loop_on_worker));
    let mut awake_min = u64::MAX;
    let mut awake_max = 0u64;
    // Steps whose awake count is not the step's body count (1240 on J-T): the sleep gate's form for
    // every program (critique W5).
    let mut asleep_steps = 0usize;
    let mut pairs_mismatch = 0usize;
    let mut constraints_mismatch = 0usize;
    let mut contacts_mismatch = 0usize;
    let mut win_sum = Counts::default();
    for (i, c) in rows
        .iter()
        .enumerate()
        .filter_map(|(i, r)| r.counts.map(|c| (i, c)))
    {
        awake_min = awake_min.min(c.awake);
        awake_max = awake_max.max(c.awake);
        asleep_steps += usize::from(c.awake != rows[i].bodies as u64);
        pairs_mismatch += usize::from(c.counter_pairs != c.bp_pairs);
        constraints_mismatch += usize::from(c.counter_constraints != c.manifolds);
        contacts_mismatch += usize::from(c.counter_contacts != c.points);
        if (args.window.0..args.window.1).contains(&i) {
            win_sum.bp_pairs += c.bp_pairs;
            win_sum.pairs_with_manifold += c.pairs_with_manifold;
            win_sum.pairs_active += c.pairs_active;
            win_sum.raw_manifolds += c.raw_manifolds;
            win_sum.raw_points += c.raw_points;
            win_sum.manifolds += c.manifolds;
            win_sum.points += c.points;
            win_sum.spec_points += c.spec_points;
            win_sum.spec_manifolds += c.spec_manifolds;
            win_sum.floor_manifolds += c.floor_manifolds;
            win_sum.floor_points += c.floor_points;
            win_sum.lateral_manifolds += c.lateral_manifolds;
            win_sum.counter_pairs += c.counter_pairs;
            win_sum.counter_constraints += c.counter_constraints;
            win_sum.counter_contacts += c.counter_contacts;
            win_sum.rows += c.rows;
            win_sum.awake += c.awake;
        }
    }
    if args.receipt {
        // J-T keeps the frozen test and message; a dynamic program wants every body of the step awake.
        if args.dyn_program.is_none() {
            if awake_min != PYRAMID_BODIES as u64 || awake_max != PYRAMID_BODIES as u64 {
                voids.push(format!(
                    "sleep: awake bodies {awake_min}..{awake_max}, want {PYRAMID_BODIES} every step"
                ));
            }
        } else if asleep_steps > 0 {
            voids.push(format!(
                "sleep: {asleep_steps} steps with an awake count other than the step's bodies (awake {awake_min}..{awake_max})"
            ));
        }
        if pairs_mismatch + constraints_mismatch + contacts_mismatch > 0 {
            voids.push(format!(
                "recount: steps where Rapier's counters disagree with the recount: pairs {pairs_mismatch}, \
                 constraints {constraints_mismatch}, contacts {contacts_mismatch}"
            ));
        }
    }
    if let Some(why) = dyn_run.as_ref().and_then(dyn_scenes::DynRun::void_reason) {
        voids.push(why);
    }
    if !voids.is_empty() {
        exit = exit.max(EXIT_VOID);
    }

    // Pose.
    let pose = pose_bytes(&world);
    let pose_hash = fnv1a64(&pose);
    if let Some(path) = &args.pose_out
        && let Err(e) = std::fs::write(path, &pose)
    {
        eprintln!("rapier-parity: writing {}: {e}", path.display());
        exit = exit.max(EXIT_USAGE);
    }
    let pose_verdict = match &args.expect_pose {
        None => "none".to_owned(),
        Some(path) => match std::fs::read(path) {
            Err(e) => {
                exit = exit.max(EXIT_USAGE);
                format!("unreadable: {e}")
            }
            Ok(want) if want == pose => "match".to_owned(),
            Ok(want) => {
                exit = exit.max(EXIT_POSE_MISMATCH);
                let first = want
                    .chunks(POSE_BYTES_PER_BODY)
                    .zip(pose.chunks(POSE_BYTES_PER_BODY))
                    .position(|(a, b)| a != b);
                format!(
                    "mismatch: {} vs {} bytes, first differing body {first:?}",
                    want.len(),
                    pose.len()
                )
            }
        },
    };
    if let Some(path) = &args.csv
        && let Err(e) = std::fs::write(path, csv_text(&rows, args.receipt))
    {
        eprintln!("rapier-parity: writing {}: {e}", path.display());
        exit = exit.max(EXIT_USAGE);
    }
    let dyn_json = match dyn_run.as_mut() {
        None => String::new(),
        Some(d) => match d.finish(&world, args.sanity.as_deref(), args.scene_dump.as_deref()) {
            Ok(json) => json,
            Err(e) => {
                eprintln!("rapier-parity: {e}");
                exit = exit.max(EXIT_USAGE);
                ",\"dyn\":null".to_owned()
            }
        },
    };

    // Summary.
    let win = &rows[args.window.0..args.window.1];
    let win_ns: u64 = win.iter().map(|r| r.wall_ns).sum();
    let win_mean = win_ns as f64 / win.len() as f64;
    let steps_per_s = win.len() as f64 / (win_ns as f64 * 1e-9);
    let last_counts = rows.last().and_then(|r| r.counts);
    let final_top_y = world
        .boxes
        .last()
        .map_or(f32::NAN, |&h| world.bodies[h].translation().y);
    let combined = CoefficientCombineRule::combine(
        FRICTION,
        FRICTION,
        CoefficientCombineRule::Max,
        CoefficientCombineRule::Max,
    );
    let logical_cores = std::thread::available_parallelism().map_or(0, std::num::NonZeroUsize::get);

    println!(
        "rapier-parity: rapier3d {RAPIER_VERSION} arm {ARM} ({SIMD_LANES} lanes) avx2 {} counters {} | cfg {} gap {} knobs [{}] workers {} install {} steps {} window {}..{}",
        cfg!(target_feature = "avx2"),
        world.pipeline.counters.enabled(),
        args.cfg.name(),
        args.gap,
        args.knobs.given.join(" "),
        args.workers,
        args.install.name(),
        args.steps,
        args.window.0,
        args.window.1,
    );
    println!(
        "scene: {} dynamic + {} fixed bodies, first {:?} last {:?}, aabb {:?}..{:?}, inv mass {} inv inertia {:?}, spawn hash {:#018x}",
        id.dynamic_bodies,
        id.fixed_bodies,
        id.first,
        id.last,
        id.aabb_min,
        id.aabb_max,
        id.inv_mass,
        id.inv_inertia,
        id.spawn_hash
    );
    if let Some(c) = &last_counts {
        println!(
            "final step: bp pairs {} pairs w/ manifold {} manifolds {} points {} (spec {}) floor {} lateral {} \
             constraints {} rows {} awake {} top_y {}",
            c.bp_pairs,
            c.pairs_with_manifold,
            c.manifolds,
            c.points,
            c.spec_points,
            c.floor_manifolds,
            c.lateral_manifolds,
            c.counter_constraints,
            c.rows,
            c.awake,
            c.top_y
        );
    }
    println!(
        "pool threads {pool_min}..{pool_max} (want {}), pose_hash {pose_hash:#018x}, expect_pose {pose_verdict}",
        args.workers
    );
    for v in &voids {
        println!("VOID: {v}");
    }

    let n_win = (args.window.1 - args.window.0) as f64;
    let mut s = String::with_capacity(4096);
    let _ = write!(
        s,
        "{{\"runner\":{},\"label\":{},\"args\":[{}],\"engine\":\"rapier3d\",\"rapier_version\":{},\
         \"arm\":\"{ARM}\",\"simd_lanes\":{SIMD_LANES},\
         \"features\":{{\"parallel\":true,\"simd8\":{},\"simd4\":{},\"enhanced_determinism\":{}}},\
         \"source_fnv1a64\":{{\"main_rs\":\"{:#018x}\",\"cargo_toml\":\"{:#018x}\",\"cargo_lock\":\"{:#018x}\",\
         \"spec_rs\":\"{:#018x}\",\"dyn_scenes_rs\":\"{:#018x}\"}},\
         \"target_features\":{{\"avx2\":{},\"fma\":{},\"bmi2\":{}}},\"debug_assertions\":{},\"target_env\":{},\
         \"scene\":\"jolt\",\"gap\":{},\"friction\":{},\"friction_combine\":\"Max\",\"friction_combined\":{},\
         \"bodies\":{},\"workers\":{},\"install\":{},\"cfg\":{},\"config\":{},\
         \"rows_formula\":\"S*(P*(Np+fib*F)+Q*(Np+F)), F=3*Nm (Simplified) or 2*Np (Coulomb)\",\
         \"scene_identity\":{{\"dynamic_bodies\":{},\"fixed_bodies\":{},\"colliders\":{},\"first\":{},\"last\":{},\
         \"first_equals_ours\":{},\"last_equals_ours\":{},\"aabb_min\":{},\"aabb_max\":{},\"aabb_equals_expected\":{},\
         \"uniform_boxes\":{},\"inv_mass\":{},\"inv_inertia\":{},\"inv_mass_ulps_vs_ours\":{},\
         \"inv_inertia_ulps_vs_ours\":[{},{},{}],\"spawn_hash\":\"{:#018x}\"}},\

#[cfg(test)]
mod tests {
    use super::*;

    /// The red control of the placement void: `loop_on_pool_worker == false` is not reachable from
    /// the command line (inside `pool.install` the loop is always on a worker), so the rule is
    /// pinned here, both ways.
    #[test]
    fn placement_void_fires_only_for_a_loop_outside_the_pool() {
        assert!(placement_void(InstallMode::Loop, false).is_some());
        assert!(placement_void(InstallMode::Loop, true).is_none());
        assert!(placement_void(InstallMode::Step, false).is_none());
        assert!(placement_void(InstallMode::Step, true).is_none());
    }
}
